# How I want to be helped

The agent copies this file to workspace/profile/preferences.md on first run. Anything left
blank uses the default named under its heading, and the result says a default was used.
Standing rules the agent learns from you are kept separately, in workspace/policies/.

## When a follow-up is due

Working days something may stay owed to me with no word before a follow-up draft is written.
Default: 5.

## What to leave out

Senders or kinds of mail the agent should ignore. Newsletters, notifications and mass mail are
left out in any case.

## What always matters

People, organisations or subjects to name whenever they appear.

## How long records are kept

Months after a commitment is closed or a report is written. The brief lists what is older for
me to remove; the agent deletes nothing itself. Default: keep everything.
